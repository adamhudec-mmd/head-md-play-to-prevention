# Get Slimed Blud (RPG Griefing Sim)


Playing as the mischievous 1lvl slime word who bullies the players in an mmorpg. The slime has fun by frustrating (bulling) the players by repeatedly spamming actions that annoy them (block a path, steal item, taunt them, etc.). These actions fill up specific bars –> you as a player are guide into a moment of reflection.
