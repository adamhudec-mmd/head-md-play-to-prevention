# Get Slimed 
*An RPG where you play the griefer, and feel what it does.*



You play a level-1 slime in an MMORPG whose only goal is to have fun by frustrating other players. You block doors, steal items, taunt, and chase them down. It is satisfying at first, then the game makes you look at what you've been doing.

*A moment of realization?*

**Slime actions (mild → malicious)**
- Block a path or door
- Steal an item
- Prevent players from fleeing a battle
- Chase them if they do escape
- Attack multiple times per turn, breaking the turn-based rules

## Stats (inspired by Undertale)
| Original | Slimed | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| MP – Mana Points | Malicious Points | how far you've pushed |
| EXP – Experience | Exiled Players | how many you've driven away |
| HP – Health | Hurt Points | the harm you've caused |
| (new) FUN bar | | Does it ever fill up? Or does it empty as the others fill? |
